How it started.

Solunera started in 2023 with one person. Kamel Nassif was studying computer science and had spent years in graphic design and print production. The shops around him needed websites, so he built them.

Then they needed menus that take orders. Then systems to run the whole day. Each client asked for the next thing, and we learned to build it.

Today a field team visits business owners and listens, a studio builds, and the systems keep running after launch.
